Help & how it works

The rules of the neighbourhood, in plain language.

🧭 How IoU works

  1. Post a task — pick a genre, describe what you need, set when it's needed. IoU suggests a fair credit offer from recent similar tasks.
  2. A neighbour offers to help — you get a message in your shared thread.
  3. Negotiate the value — both sides propose a value and a buffer. If the ranges overlap, IoU settles at the middle of the overlap. IoU suggests a compromise after every round. You have 3 days before the request reopens.
  4. Credits held safely — when the task starts, the agreed credits are set aside by IoU. Neither side can spend them until the task is settled.
  5. Both confirm — credits are released to the helper only when both sides confirm completion.
  6. Kudos — happy with the help? Send +10% as a thank-you.

📜 Community terms

  • Credits are a record of favours, not money. They can't be bought, sold or cashed out.
  • Your balance can go as low as -20 credits — that's the community trusting you to give back.
  • One hour of your time is worth what the two of you agree it's worth. Negotiate fairly.
  • Your HKID is only used to verify residency and is never shown to other users.
  • Be honest in disputes: moderators can refund credits and repeated abuse gets you removed.
  • Meet in safe, public places when possible. IoU connects neighbours — it can't supervise them.

🔄 Optional task chains

IoU can notice several open tasks with similar credit values and invite their owners to join a voluntary chain. You do not need to list a skill: you choose whether you are comfortable completing one of the other tasks.

  • Joining is optional. Declining does not affect your ordinary task.
  • A chain activates once at least three invited participants accept.
  • Each participant completes one other participant's task and has their own task completed by someone else.
  • No credits move just because you join. Every task still uses its normal agreement, safely held payment, proof, completion, and problem-reporting process.

⚖️ Published dispute rules

  • Eligible tasks have a three-day dispute window after completion confirmation.
  • Evidence should show the problem and may include up to five screened files.
  • Participants should first discuss a refund, redo/fix, or another clearly described compensation arrangement.
  • IoU may suggest a fair solution, but both participants must accept before credits are redistributed.
  • A moderator may grant no refund, a partial refund, a full refund, or keep credits frozen while requesting more evidence.
  • Moderator decisions are recorded in the credit history and update balances automatically.

❓ Frequently asked questions

What if we can't agree on a value?

After 3 days without an overlap, the helper is released and your request goes back to the open board — no hard feelings, no credits moved.

Where do my first credits come from?

Everyone starts with 10 welcome credits. After that, you earn by helping others — or spend into the -20 floor while you find your feet.

What happens if the task goes wrong?

Within 3 days of completion, the requester can open a dispute with evidence. You can negotiate a refund between yourselves first; if that fails, a moderator decides.

How is the suggested offer calculated?

From the median value of similar completed tasks in the last 90 days, adjusted for estimated effort, complexity and past quality. It's advice, not a rule — but posting far outside it needs a confirmation.

Can I change my skills or address later?

Yes — everything lives on your Account page. Better skills info means better ★ For you recommendations on the Community board.
