How this Circle works

Everyone picks exactly one task belonging to somebody else. When those choices form a closed loop, the Circle starts and no base credits move. You help one neighbour, another helps you.

Each task keeps IoU's normal safeguards: chat, agreement, completion, proof and problem reporting. Only the payment disappears.
